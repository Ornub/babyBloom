Call Doctor Immediately If

Severe abdominal pain · Heavy bleeding · Fever >100.4°F · Severe headache + vision changes · Baby stops moving (wk 28+) · Vomiting 10+/day · Fainting

Is daily vomiting normal in first trimester?
Yes — 70–80% experience nausea (wk 6–14) from hCG hormone surges. Usually improves by wk 14. Call doctor if 8–10+/day or unable to keep fluids down.
How much water should I drink daily?
8–10 glasses (2–2.5L) minimum. More if hot weather or vomiting. Include coconut water, lemon water, watermelon for variety and electrolytes.
Can I sleep on my back?
T1: Any position fine. After week 20: left side is best — improves blood flow to baby and kidneys. Use a pillow between knees for comfort.
When will I feel baby kick?
First pregnancy: 18–22 weeks (feels like butterflies or bubbles). By week 28, kicks are regular and strong. Lie on left side after eating to feel movements better!
What foods to completely avoid?
Raw/undercooked meat or eggs · Unpasteurized dairy · Raw sprouts · Alcohol · High-mercury fish (shark, swordfish). Limit caffeine to under 200mg/day.
Is exercise safe during pregnancy?
Yes! Walking, prenatal yoga, swimming are ideal. Avoid contact sports, hot yoga, heavy lifting. Stop immediately if dizzy, in pain, or bleeding.
Why am I so tired all the time?
Your body is building a placenta + increasing blood volume by 50% + surging with progesterone. Rest is productive. Eat iron-rich foods and nap when possible.
How to reduce foot swelling?
Elevate feet when sitting · Sleep on left side · Reduce salt · Drink MORE water · Comfortable shoes · Ask Arnab for foot massage nightly! Call doctor if sudden severe swelling with headache (preeclampsia warning).
